Stockpile Strategist · a game about the minerals inside everyday technology

In 52 days, China could cut off minerals US factories can't work without.

You run America's $12 billion emergency stockpile. Your job is to keep the factories running.

1 · The problem

A few minerals, like dysprosium for electric-car motors and antimony for artillery shells, come mostly from China. Since 2023, China has repeatedly restricted selling them to the US.

2 · The deadline

In November 2025, China agreed to pause its toughest new restrictions for a year. That pause has been extended only to 10 January 2027. Nobody knows what happens next.

3 · Your job

Pick one mineral and find where its supply could break. Then store reserves and sign deals, and see how your plan holds up against what really happened in 2023–26 and against 1,000 possible futures.

How big a shortage would stop this factory?
